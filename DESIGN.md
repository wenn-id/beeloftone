# Beeloft One / produksi

Direction source: the user's Beeloft One blueprint, with dark ink, restrained gold rules, light paper surfaces and readable operational tables. Antislop during implementation was explicitly chosen by the user.

ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1.

- Ink and gold echo the supplied blueprint; gold marks primary actions and the active workspace.
- Light background is the default for reading quantities on the production floor. A dark toggle supports different lighting; the two themes share contrast-tested tokens.
- Segoe UI follows the Windows environment and reads clearly in dense forms. A Georgia wordmark reflects the document's editorial character without importing fonts.
- The order ledger is the primary surface. A compact summary strip supports triage, while a full-width order detail shows quantities and history. No decorative chart or imagery.
- Numbered stage positions follow the actual manufacturing process; rework and reject are explicitly labeled exceptions, not extra steps in a linear progress bar.
- Controls have 44px minimum touch area. Order rows become vertically grouped records on mobile. Native dialogs provide focus containment and Escape handling.
- Radius: 4px fields/buttons, 8px dialogs. Shadows only separate dialogs from the working surface.
- All facts come from the existing database. Demo orders keep their DEMO labels. No fabricated business metrics.
- Text and status labels remain the primary signals; color never carries status alone. Transitions are brief button-state changes and respect reduced motion.
