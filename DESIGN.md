# Beeloft One / operations workspace

Direction source: the user's Shopeers dashboard reference and explicit Phase 1 brief. Antislop applies during implementation.

ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1.

- Beeloft One is an operational decision workspace for Indonesian production and management teams. The interface should feel precise, calm, and ready for dense daily use.
- A cool light-gray canvas separates the application frame from white working surfaces. Strong blue marks the active destination and primary action because those are the moments that need immediate recognition.
- Near-black navy text and cool slate secondary text support long sessions with real operational data. Red, amber, and green remain semantic status colors and always appear with text labels.
- Segoe UI stays as the primary typeface because it is native to the target Windows environment, readable at compact sizes, and adds no font dependency. The wordmark keeps its restrained serif treatment as the product identity motif.
- The desktop shell uses a narrow white sidebar and compact utility header. The sidebar groups existing destinations by real workflow without inventing routes. On narrow screens it becomes a labeled disclosure menu that reflows above the content.
- The Command Center exists to answer one question: what needs a decision today. The `Perlu perhatian` queue is the dominant surface; KPIs and business snapshots provide context and remain visually secondary.
- Cards use a 14px radius, controls use 9px, and status badges use a capsule only because they label real states. Shadows are reserved for raised decision surfaces and dialogs; most surfaces rely on borders.
- Spacing follows a compact 4px-based scale. Information density stays high, but major sections use larger gaps so management priorities remain easy to scan.
- Motion is limited to hover, focus, and mobile navigation state changes. Reduced-motion preferences remove non-essential transitions.
- All numbers and labels come from existing API responses. No decorative charts, invented trends, placeholder identities, or new business metrics are introduced.
- Every interactive control keeps at least a 44px touch area, visible focus, keyboard operation, mobile reflow, and usable text at 200% zoom.
